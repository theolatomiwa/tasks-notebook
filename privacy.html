Tasks Notebook

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

The short version
  • We only collect what we need to sign you in and keep your tasks in sync.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. We never sell your data.
  • You can use the app without an account; then your tasks never leave your browser.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it at any time.

01Who we are

Tasks Notebook (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a to-do app at tasks-notebook.vercel.app, built and run by Olatomiwa Olarinde as an individual. We are the data controller for the personal information described here. You can reach us at olatomiwaolarinde@gmail.com.

02What we collect

If you use the app as a guest (without signing in), we collect nothing on our servers. Your tasks, timer history and settings are saved only in your browser’s local storage on that device.

If you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign up with a password, it is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it.
  • Google profile details, only if you choose “Continue with Google”: your name, email address and profile photo link. We do not get access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or anything else in your Google account.
  • Your content: the tasks, steps, notes, due dates, urgent flags and names of people you tag, so we can sync them across your devices.
  • Technical data: our hosting and database providers automatically record basic request logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) to keep the service running and secure.

Names of other people you tag on tasks (for example, who a task is for) are part of your content. Please only add what you are comfortable storing.

03How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To store your tasks and sync them between your devices.
  • To send account emails you ask for: confirming your email, sign-in links and password resets. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We process this information because it is needed to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract) and, where relevant, with your consent, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.

05What’s stored on your device

The app uses your browser’s local storage, not tracking cookies, to keep your tasks available offline, remember your timer and settings, and keep you signed in. When you sign out, your account’s tasks are removed from that device (unless some changes have not reached the server yet). Clearing your browser data removes everything stored locally.

06How long we keep it

We keep your account and tasks for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and all your tasks within 30 days. Provider logs and backups are kept for a short period by those providers and then deleted on their normal schedule.

07Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • have your account and data deleted;
  • object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email olatomiwaolarinde@gmail.com from the address on your account. We will reply within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) or your local data protection authority.

08How we protect it

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Your tasks are stored with database rules that let only your signed-in account read or change them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

09Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we will let signed-in users know in the app.
